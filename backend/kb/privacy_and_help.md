# Privacy and Getting Started

## Deleting personal data
Customers can request deletion of their personal data from Account Settings > Privacy Center > Delete My Data. A confirmation email is sent to the registered address and the request is completed within 30 days. Deleting data permanently removes the account, order history and saved preferences, and this cannot be undone. Invoices and records that must be kept for legal and tax reasons are retained for the legally required period. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.

## Getting started
The Help Center getting-started guide walks through the first steps: creating a workspace, inviting team members and assigning roles under Settings > Team, and creating a first project from the Home screen. Reporting is covered in the Reports section of the guide, where each report type has a short step-by-step tutorial. Customers who need hands-on help can book a free onboarding session from the Help Center.
