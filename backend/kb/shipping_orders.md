# Shipping and Orders

## Tracking an order
A shipping confirmation email with a tracking link is sent as soon as an order ships. Customers can also open Orders > My Orders and select Track Package. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days; if a package has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support so a carrier investigation can be opened.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item that was ordered, customers can start a return from Orders > My Orders > Return or Replace Item. Please include a photo of the damage or of the packing label. A replacement is shipped at no cost, and a prepaid return label is provided by email. Returns for other reasons are accepted within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed from Orders > My Orders > Edit Address, but only while the order status is Processing. Once an order has shipped the address can no longer be changed in the system; customers should then contact the carrier using the tracking link to request a redirect, or refuse delivery so the item is returned.
