# Technical Troubleshooting

## App crashes, freezes or loads slowly
First make sure the app is updated to the latest version from the app store. Then clear the app cache: on Android go to Settings > Apps > select the app > Storage > Clear Cache; on iPhone offload and reinstall the app, which keeps account data in the cloud. Restart the phone and try again. If the app still crashes, send support the device model, OS version and app version found under App > Settings > About.

## Service outages and server errors
Errors such as 500 or 503, or pages that never load, often mean a service outage. Check the service status page for active incidents. During an incident no action is needed from the customer, and the service normally recovers on its own; customers can retry after the incident is marked resolved. If the status page shows no incident, try a different network or a private browser window and contact support if the problem continues.

## Device not connecting or pairing
If a device shows as offline, restart the device by holding the power button for 10 seconds, keep it within 3 meters of the phone, and make sure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are on. Then open the app, remove the device under Devices > Remove, and add it again with Add Device to pair it from scratch. The device only connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. If pairing times out repeatedly, perform a factory reset by holding the reset button for 15 seconds.
