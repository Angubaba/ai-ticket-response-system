# Billing and Payments

## Duplicate charges and refunds
If a customer was charged twice for the same order or subscription period, they can request a refund from Billing > Billing History by selecting the duplicate transaction and choosing Request Refund. Duplicate charges that are confirmed are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Pending card authorizations that appear twice usually drop off by themselves within 3 business days and are not real charges.

## Failed payments and updating the payment method
When a payment fails, the most common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or the bank declining the transaction. Customers can add or replace a card under Billing > Payment Methods, then select Retry Payment on the failed invoice. The subscription stays active for a 7-day grace period after a failed renewal. If the card is valid and payments still fail, the customer should contact their bank to allow the transaction.

## Downloading invoices and receipts
Invoices are available as PDFs under Billing > Billing History. Select the invoice and choose Download. To include a company name or tax ID on future invoices, add them under Billing > Billing Details before the next billing date; invoices that were already issued cannot be edited, but support can reissue them on request.

## Cancelling a subscription
A subscription can be cancelled at any time from Account Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and will not renew afterwards. Cancelling does not automatically produce a refund for the current period. Customers can reactivate the subscription at any time before the period ends from the same page.
