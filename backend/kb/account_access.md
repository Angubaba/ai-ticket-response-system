# Account Access

## Resetting a forgotten password
Customers can reset their passwords using the Forgot Password option on the login page. Enter the registered email address and a password reset link will be sent to it. The link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email is not received, customers should check their spam folder or contact support. Passwords must be at least 10 characters long.

## Unlocking a locked account
After 5 failed login attempts an account is locked for security reasons. The lock lifts automatically after 30 minutes. To unlock sooner, customers can use the Unlock Account link in the lock notification email and verify their identity by confirming the registered email address and the last four digits of the phone number on file. If the notification email is not available, contact support.

## Changing the email address on an account
The email address can be changed from Account Settings > Profile > Email. A verification link is sent to the new address and the change takes effect once that link is clicked. Until it is verified, the old email address remains active. If the old email is no longer accessible, contact support so that identity can be verified manually.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA) problems
If the authenticator app codes are rejected, first check that the phone's date and time are set to automatic, since time drift causes invalid codes. Customers who lost their device can sign in using one of the single-use backup codes that were shown when 2FA was enabled, then set up 2FA again from Account Settings > Security. If backup codes are also unavailable, contact support to start a manual identity verification, which can take up to 2 business days.
